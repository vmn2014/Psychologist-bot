# CBT / КПТ

## What is CBT?

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) is a structured, time-limited, goal-oriented psychotherapy that focuses on the relationship between thoughts, feelings, and behaviors.

## Core Principles

1. **Thoughts affect feelings**: Negative thoughts lead to negative emotions
2. **Feelings affect behavior**: Negative emotions lead to unhelpful behaviors
3. **Behavior reinforces thoughts**: Unhelpful behaviors reinforce negative thoughts

## Common Techniques

### Thought Record
| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Situation | What happened? |
| Automatic Thought | What went through your mind? |
| Emotion | What did you feel? (0-10) |
| Evidence For | Facts supporting the thought |
| Evidence Against | Facts against the thought |
| Balanced Thought | More balanced perspective |
| Action | What can you do now? |

### Cognitive Distortions
- All-or-nothing thinking
- Overgeneralization
- Mental filter
- Disqualifying the positive
- Mind reading
- Fortune telling
- Catastrophizing
- Emotional reasoning
- Should statements
- Labeling

## Safety Notes

- CBT is evidence-based for mild to moderate depression and anxiety
- For severe conditions, professional supervision is required
- This bot provides psychoeducation and self-help exercises only
- Not a replacement for professional CBT therapy
