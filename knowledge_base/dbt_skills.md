# DBT Skills

## Core Modules

### 1. Mindfulness
- Observe: Notice without reacting
- Describe: Put words to experience
- Participate: Engage fully in the moment
- Non-judgmental stance
- One-mindfully
- Effectively

### 2. Distress Tolerance

#### TIPP Skills
- **T**emperature: Cold water on face
- **I**ntense exercise: Brief physical activity
- **P**aced breathing: Slow, controlled breaths
- **P**rogressive muscle relaxation

#### STOP Skill
- **S**top: Don't react immediately
- **T**ake a breath: Breathe deeply
- **O**bserve: What's happening inside and out?
- **P**roceed mindfully: Choose your action

### 3. Emotion Regulation
- Check the facts
- Opposite action
- Problem solving
- ABC: Accumulate positives, Build mastery, Cope ahead
- PLEASE: treat Physical illness, balanced Eating, avoid mood-Altering substances, balanced Sleep, balanced Exercise

### 4. Interpersonal Effectiveness
- DEAR MAN: Describe, Express, Assert, Reinforce, Mindful, Appear confident, Negotiate
- GIVE: Gentle, Interested, Validate, Easy manner
- FAST: Fair, Apologies (no excessive), Stick to values, Truthful

## Safety Notes

- DBT skills are helpful for emotional regulation
- For BPD or severe conditions, professional DBT program is needed
- Crisis skills are for temporary stabilization, not long-term treatment
