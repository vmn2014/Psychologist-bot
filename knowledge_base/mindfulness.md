# Mindfulness & Grounding

## 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Technique

Use when: anxiety, overwhelm, dissociation, panic (without medical red flags)

1. **5 things you see**: Name 5 objects around you
2. **4 things you feel**: Touch 4 textures
3. **3 things you hear**: Listen for 3 sounds
4. **2 things you smell**: Notice 2 scents
5. **1 thing you taste**: Notice 1 taste

## Breathing 4-6

- Inhale for 4 counts
- Exhale for 6 counts
- Repeat 5 cycles

**Warning**: Not a medical recommendation for chest pain, fainting, severe shortness of breath.

## Body Scan

1. Lie down comfortably
2. Start at toes, move up to head
3. Notice sensations without judgment
4. Breathe into tense areas

## Thought Defusion

- "I notice I'm having the thought that..."
- "My mind is telling me that..."
- "I'm having the feeling that..."

## Safety Notes

- Mindfulness is evidence-based for anxiety and stress
- For trauma or psychosis, professional guidance needed
- Stop if you feel worse or overwhelmed
